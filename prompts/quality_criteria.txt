Requirements quality checklist:

1. The requirements must include clear goals and objectives.
2. Stakeholders must have names, roles, and needs.
3. Functional requirements must include an id, statement, actor, priority, and acceptance criteria.
4. Functional requirements should be clear enough to test.
5. Non-functional requirements must include a category, statement, priority, and measurable metric.
6. Data requirements must include entity name, fields, purpose, and sensitivity.
7. Constraints should describe scope, technology, time, legal, or business limits.
8. Success criteria must include a statement, measure, and target value.
9. Risks and assumptions should be recorded.
10. Priorities should show what is must, should, could, or optional.
11. Open unresolved questions must be treated as gaps.
12. Vague words such as easy, quickly, fast, secure, reliable, simple, good, accurate, or user-friendly should be clarified.
13. Missing metrics, missing targets, missing actors, missing data fields, and open questions should reduce the quality score.
14. The output must include useful follow-up questions that can help identify missing or unclear requirement details.
15. The requirements are ready for the next stage only when the important sections are complete and no serious gaps remain.
